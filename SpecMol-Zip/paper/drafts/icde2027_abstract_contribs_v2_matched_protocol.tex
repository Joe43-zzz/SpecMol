\begin{abstract}
Pretrained graph neural networks for molecular property prediction
routinely incorporate three-dimensional structural information through
pair representations, yet whether this geometry contributes anything to
downstream performance under matched evaluation has not been
systematically audited. We conduct a controlled empirical study of three
independently motivated 3D-pair injection mechanisms---static bond-level
gating (V2-T5), iterative pair-node update (T6), and pair-biased
multi-head attention with a per-head sigmoid gate (T7)---within a shared
ChebNet II spectral backbone trained with NT-Xent contrastive
pretraining. We evaluate on six MoleculeNet-derived benchmarks under a
matched Uni-Mol scaffold-fold protocol with three to nine training seeds
per cell: BBBP, BACE, and ClinTox for classification, and FreeSolv,
ESOL, and Lipo for regression. \emph{Across all six benchmarks, the
three deep variants tie or trail the V0 2D baseline within seed
variance at matched split.} The FreeSolv result, previously reported in
the literature as a measurable geometry win for V2-T5 + Uni-Mol pair
($\approx 0.04$ RMSE over the 30-seed RF baseline of
\citet{deng2023systematic}), does not survive a matched-V0 control: at
identical scaffold split, identical Uni-Mol pair data, and identical
seeds, V0 reaches $0.665 \pm 0.031$ RMSE vs.\ V2-T5
$0.675 \pm 0.036$ on FreeSolv, while ESOL and Lipo collapse cleanly to
V0 within seed variance. Classical Random Forest with Morgan + 11
RDKit 2D descriptors dominates BACE by approximately 13 AUC points and
the largest regression benchmark, Lipo, by approximately $0.18$ RMSE,
demonstrating fingerprint saturation as the dominant ceiling on
multiple endpoints. A mechanism-level analysis of V2-T5 reveals an
aromatic-routing pattern (Pearson $r{=}{+}0.66$) consistent across all
audited training seeds on BBBP, and T7's per-head attention gate stays
in the near-identity-residual regime documented by
ReZero~\cite{bachlechner2021rezero} and LayerScale~\cite{touvron2021cait}
across every audited (benchmark, seed) checkpoint --- characterizing a
``mechanism without metric'' regime in pretrained molecular GNNs:
learned 3D-integration mechanisms are interpretable, robust, and
consistent across seeds, yet do not translate to predictive improvement
once a matched 2D control is in place. We additionally document and
quantify a silent unidirectional-bond featurization regression in our
codebase that drops approximately 19\% of heavy atoms from the symmetric
Laplacian, shifting apparent V2-T5 BACE performance by 7.4 AUC points
under unfair pre-fix comparison, and we audit fourteen widely cited
public molecular GNN repositories for the same vulnerability. We
release the full evaluation harness, all dataset processing pipelines,
baseline implementations, and per-seed checkpoints.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(C1) A matched-V0 audit of 3D-pair injection across six
benchmarks.} Under a single matched Uni-Mol scaffold-fold protocol with
$n{=}3$--$9$ training seeds per cell, the three deep variants we test
(V2-T5, T6, T7) tie or trail a 2D-only baseline (V0) on every
classification \emph{and} every regression benchmark within seed
variance. The FreeSolv $-0.04$ RMSE separation that we initially read as
a real geometry win does not survive a matched-V0 control on the same
data: V0 reaches RMSE $0.665 \pm 0.031$ vs.\ V2-T5
$0.675 \pm 0.036$ on FreeSolv at $n{=}3$. Random Forest with Morgan +
RDKit-2D descriptors dominates BACE by $\sim 13$ AUC and Lipo by
$\sim 0.18$ RMSE, identifying fingerprint saturation as the dominant
ceiling on multiple endpoints.

\item \textbf{(C2) V2-T5 learns aromatic-routing on BBBP even when test
AUC does not improve.} Across all nine audited BBBP V2-T5 checkpoints,
the trained gate up-weights aromatic bonds with Pearson
$r \in [0.62, 0.69]$ against the bond-aromatic indicator. The mechanism
is reproducible across seeds and chemically interpretable, but it does
not translate into a population-level test-AUC improvement over V0 ---
the central ``mechanism without metric'' observation of this paper.

\item \textbf{(C3) T7's per-head attention gate stays in the
near-identity regime.} Across all nine audited (BBBP, BACE, FreeSolv
$\times$ seeds 9/19/29) T7 checkpoints, the per-head sigmoid attention
gate $\sigma(\gamma_h)$ stays within $[0.122, 0.124]$ after $1000$
pretraining epochs (initialization at $\sigma({-}2)\approx 0.119$). This
places trained T7 in the near-identity-residual regime documented by
ReZero~\cite{bachlechner2021rezero} and
LayerScale~\cite{touvron2021cait}, and is consistent with the
contrastive pretraining objective providing little direct gradient
pressure on attention-output utilization.

\item \textbf{(C4) Forensic featurization audit.} An internal
unidirectional-bond featurization regression in our GraphDTA-derived
loader~\cite{nguyen2021graphdta} caused approximately $19\%/18\%/24\%$
of heavy atoms on BBBP/BACE/FreeSolv to receive row-out-degree zero in
the symmetric Laplacian. Controlling for this regression on BACE
shifts apparent V2-T5 by $7.4$ AUC points --- more than the entire
deep-vs-classical gap on the same benchmark. We audit fourteen widely
cited public molecular GNN repositories for the same vulnerability and
report where it is and is not present
(\S\ref{sec:featurization-control}); the audit script
(\texttt{paper/audit\_b4\_atom\_dropout.py}) is released as a
defensive check.

\item \textbf{(C5) Reproducibility artifact.} Per the ICDE 2027 EAB
requirement, we release the full evaluation harness, all dataset
preparation scripts, baseline implementations (RF + Chemprop), per-seed
result JSONs, and the per-seed pretraining checkpoints. All numbers in
our tables are regenerated by a single
\texttt{paper/make\_tables.py} from machine-readable per-seed JSON,
and the end-to-end pipeline from raw SMILES to LaTeX table reproduces
in under $\sim 100$~GPU-hours plus $\sim 6$~CPU-hours on a single user's
allocation.
\end{itemize}
