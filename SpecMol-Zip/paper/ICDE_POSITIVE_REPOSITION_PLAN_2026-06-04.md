## 2. Final positive thesis

> Geometry-aware pretrained molecular encoders, when **properly end-to-end finetuned** rather than read out by a
> frozen linear probe, are **competitive with or beat a strong matched fingerprint+RF baseline on the NON-SATURATED
> subset** of standard benchmarks (BBBP and FreeSolv confirmed; ClinTox/Lipo pending the real Uni-Mol finetune), even
> though the cheap **frozen 3D-pair injection signal itself adds nothing** (ties a shape-matched random-pair null on
> every cell). The positive deliverable is a matched-protocol diagnostic — random-pair null + frozen-probe→finetune
> correction + matched-split RF ceiling — that tells practitioners WHERE deep geometry-aware models are the right tool
> (non-saturated, recovered only by finetuning) and WHERE a descriptor RF already saturates (BACE/ESOL/QM7). The
> positive is the finetuned ENCODER's capacity, not this paper's spectral injection mechanism nor "geometry helps."
> RMSE is on standardized labels and never compared to the kcal/mol literature.
