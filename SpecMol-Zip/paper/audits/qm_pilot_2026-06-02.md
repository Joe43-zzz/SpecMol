# QM geometry-signal pilot — verdict (2026-06-02)

**Question.** Does Uni-Mol 3D pair-repr injection extract a *real* geometry signal where geometry **is** the signal (QM9 dipole μ), and does it beat the fingerprint/descriptor ceiling? Two targets span the spectrum: QM7 atomization energy (composition-dominated) and QM9 μ (geometry-pure). 7 arms isolate geometry from architecture via pair-shuffle controls; a **matched** RF (deep's exact split) measures the cheap ceiling.

> ⚠️ **Coordinate caveat (read first).** This pilot fed Uni-Mol **RDKit-MMFF conformers, not the true GDB-9 DFT coordinates** (`prepare_qm9mu_unimol_input.py:98` `EmbedMolecule(randomSeed=42)` + `:102` `MMFFOptimizeMolecule`). So every "geometry" result below is **MMFF geometry**, not DFT geometry — and Hamakawa2025 / Adams&Coley show approximate conformers can collapse the geometry signal. The genuine make-or-break cell (Uni-Mol fed **true DFT coords**, end-to-end finetune) is **not done here**; it is the Stage-2 deciding experiment in `shiny-hugging-glacier.md`. Read "real geometry" / "reads geometry" below as "reads MMFF geometry," and treat the −0.033 T8 effect as a **lower-ish bound** that DFT coords may amplify or wash out.

## What it means

1. **QM7 (composition).** Deep's best arm T7 beats V0 by −11.7, but **~92% is architecture** (T7random alone −10.8, real geometry only −0.9). T8 splits 5.1 arch + 3.5 geom. And a count/composition RF (99–105) **crushes** every deep arm (137–149) by ~38 — atomization energy ≈ Σ atomic contributions, so 3D adds almost nothing on top of composition.

2. **QM9-μ (geometry-pure).** This is the one place a geometry signal shows up: **only T8** reads it — T8 0.966 ≪ T8random 0.999 ≈ V0 0.993, a geometry-**isolated** −0.033 (~3.3% rel, ~3σ); T8's architecture alone (+0.006) does *not* help, so the gain is genuinely the Uni-Mol pair-repr's (MMFF) geometry, not the architecture. T7/V2T5/standard random ≈ V0. **But** a charge-aware strong RF (0.925) still **beats** the best deep arm (T8 0.966) by 0.042 — even geometry-pure μ does not break the descriptor ceiling, because Gasteiger partial charges are a cheap 2D proxy for the electron distribution that sets the dipole. (Whether **DFT** coords push T8 below the 0.925 RF ceiling is the open Stage-2 question.)

## Paper implication (strengthens C1 + a precise positive sub-finding)

> Across the geometry-signal spectrum — saturated ADMET (prior work) → composition-dominated QM7 → geometry-pure QM9 dipole — a fingerprint/descriptor RF matches or beats deep 3D-injection. 3D injection extracts a geometry signal **only** on the geometry-pure dipole and **only** through the richest mechanism (T8 atom↔pair co-update; ~3% RMSE, geometry-isolated, ~3σ), and even there stays **below** a cheap charge-aware RF. The injection *mechanism* matters (T8 ≫ T7 ≫ scalar gate for reading geometry), but the practical ceiling is still a strong baseline. **Caveat:** measured on MMFF (not DFT) conformers; the true-DFT-coord cell that would test whether real geometry clears the ceiling is the unmade Stage-2 deciding experiment.
